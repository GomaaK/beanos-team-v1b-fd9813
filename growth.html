The growth engine

Wi-Fi sign-in, the app and our data.

Owning the relationship with our guests is the biggest lever in the plan. We turn the Wi-Fi password into the first step, and we make the most of the data Beano's already holds.

The idea

Guests get the Wi-Fi password by signing in through the Beano's app.

Every guest who stays for hours becomes a known guest: an installed app, a consented phone number and a reason to come back. It gives us what the delivery apps keep to themselves.

Why it fits Beano's

  • Guests already stay for hours: laptop workers at U Venues, 90th Street, Makram Ebeid and Zamalek, and families at District 5. F1
  • No loyalty or app at any branch we visited, and delivery apps hold the guest data. F1
  • Euromonitor: under inflation, guests keep visiting with smaller baskets, so frequency is the lever to protect. S8

How we make it work: three stages

Wi-Fi sign-in page

No install. Phone number plus a clear opt-in. Pilot at 90th Street and Makram Ebeid (Week 6 in the 90-day plan).

Measure: sign-ins as a share of Wi-Fi sessions; opt-in rate (proposed 30% by Month 3).

The Beano's app as the faster way in

One tap to the Wi-Fi, plus an instant reward such as a first-drink treat and the Morning Club (8th breakfast free). Guests who prefer not to install can still sign in on the web page.

Measure: install rate, second visit within 14 days, repeat rate.

App-first Wi-Fi where it works

Only in the work-heavy branches, and only after stages 1 and 2 prove conversion without hurting reviews. Tourists and one-time guests keep a simple path.

Decision gate in Q2 2027, based on the pilot data.

Why stages: an app install before Wi-Fi adds friction. A guest who cannot connect fast posts a bad review. Starting with a simple sign-in, then making the app the better reward, protects the experience while we build the list. Egypt-specific evidence for app-gated Wi-Fi is thin, so this is a pilot with clear exit numbers, not a promise. ZUS Coffee in Malaysia reports about 70% of sales through its app S18, but it is a different market and a vendor source.

The legal guardrail we must build in

Egypt's data protection law is now live

  • Personal Data Protection Law 151 of 2020; Executive Regulations issued in November 2025 with a one-year grace period, so full enforcement is expected around now. S22S25
  • Consent must be explicit, informed, specific, in Arabic, and separate from terms and privacy notices. S22
  • Most controllers need a licence from the Personal Data Protection Center, and direct electronic marketing needs its own licence or permit. S24S25

What that means for us

  • A separate, unticked marketing opt-in on the Wi-Fi page and in the app. S23
  • Keep consent records for three years, and make withdrawing easy. S23S24
  • Notify the Center within 72 hours of a breach. S24
  • Check with Beano's legal counsel whether Wi-Fi access can depend on sharing a phone number, and offer a minimal path if not.

We are not lawyers. These are law-firm summaries and need confirmation with Beano's counsel before launch. Decree references differ between firms.

Making the most of the data Beano's already holds

Step one is to bring what exists into one view and classify it. We have not seen this data yet, so everything below is the plan to use it.

What we hold

  • POS: sales by branch, item and hour
  • Delivery apps: orders, ratings, reviews
  • The 15585 hotline
  • Google, elmenus and Instagram reviews and insights
  • The 2017 app and its guest data
  • La Poire group: bakery and sister-brand sales
  • From now: Wi-Fi sign-ins and the app

How we classify it

  • Guests by recency, frequency and spend
  • Time of day: morning, lunch, afternoon, evening
  • Branch role: neighbourhood, mall, GEM
  • Products: stars, plowhorses, puzzles, dogs
  • Channel: dine-in, delivery, retail
  • Guest type: work, family, student, social, tourist

What we do with it

  • Morning regulars → the Morning Club
  • Laptop crowd → the Work Bundle
  • Families → kids' menu
  • Gen Z → Signature drops and TikTok
  • Lapsed for 30 days → a win-back message
  • GEM visitors → souvenir retail

First 30 days

  • Pull the five internal numbers: revenue by branch, sales and margin by item, average check and channel split, the 2017 app and its data, and the budget and sign-off rules.
  • Build one simple dashboard (a spreadsheet or a free reporting tool is enough to start).
  • Set baselines for repeat rate, opt-in, average check and drink wait.

Success looks like

  • Members at 15% of weekly guests by Day 90 (proposed)
  • Repeat rate up 10 points in 12 months (proposed)
  • Every campaign aimed at a named segment, with a result we can read
