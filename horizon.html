Our next horizon

If the budget is open, how far can Beano's go?

Story one is closing the real gaps with a light budget in 2027. Story two is the bigger Beano's. Each step sits behind a gate, and the return is a range you can test with your own numbers.

What the open-budget scenario covers

More branches

Heliopolis (no branch today), a Sahel summer pop-up first, a second drive-through, and a refurbished pilot branch.

Gate: payback within 36 months (proposed).

Retail and gifting

An eye-level retail wall, mini-cake gift bags, a GEM souvenir range and gift boxes.

Gate: retail at 5% of sales by 2028 (proposed).

A tested market abroad

Not before 2028, in a market we test first. Egyptian specialty operators are starting to look abroad, but we have only seen one headline, so we treat it as unproven. S7

Gate: 24 months to profit in the test market (proposed).

Market backdrop: branded coffee outlets in Egypt 579 in 2023, forecast 831 by 2027 S7; new outlets continue to open and delivery efficiency matters S8. Scale today: 22 cafés on Beano's own map F2 against 40 locations on La Poire's page S1. We will agree the count with you.
