The gap analysis

Nine gaps we can close together.

What we saw, where we want to be, how we close it. Targets are proposals until the Day-30 baselines exist.

G1

Coffee quality and consistency

Fix now

Cortado 2.5, 3.5, 4, 4, 4 out of 5; none reached 5.

Tap for target, first move and measure
Where we want to be
4/5 or better in every branch, served in 5 minutes or less.
How we close it
Recalibrate grind and extraction; a weekly taste check; then review beans and roast. Bean prices are easing globally, but Egyptian landed cost has to be confirmed with purchasing.
Owner
Kitchen and purchasing
Measure
4/5 or better; drink wait 5 min or less (proposed)
Evidence
F1S11S12
G2

Service speed and accuracy

Fix now

Drink waits of 3 to 13 minutes; forgotten ashtray, coconut water and drink; fixes taking from 1 to 33 minutes.

Tap for target, first move and measure
Where we want to be
Drinks first, an order check when food lands, and any problem solved in 15 minutes or less.
How we close it
Drinks-first rule; order check; apologise, replace, manager visits.
Owner
Branch managers
Measure
100% of branches; resolved in 15 min or less (proposed)
Evidence

G9

Data we already hold is not classified

Plan

POS, delivery apps, the hotline, reviews, Instagram and the 2017 app all hold guest and sales data. We have not seen it used or joined up.

Tap for target, first move and measure
Where we want to be
One view of who comes, when, what they buy and who has stopped coming.
How we close it
A 30-day data audit; classify guests and products; build one dashboard; feed the segments to the Morning Club and campaigns.
Owner
Brand manager, IT and finance
Measure
Every KPI has a baseline by Day 30 (proposed)
Evidence
F1S8

Fix now needs no budget and starts in week 1. Plan needs design, a pilot or a decision. The gaps come from six visits by one visitor, so they are indicative until the full audit. What turns each gap into money is the five internal numbers: revenue by branch, sales and margin by item, average check and channel split, what happened to the 2017 app and its data, and the marketing budget and sign-off rules.
